Prototype limitation: This version recognizes static, one-hand poses that you teach it. It does not yet understand full sign-language grammar, two-hand signs, facial expressions, motion-based signs, or every ISL/ASL sign. A real-world translator needs a validated, consented dataset and extensive testing with sign-language users.
